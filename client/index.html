What is Mental Wellness?

It is a state of well-being where an individual realizes their potential, copes with normal stresses, works productively, and contributes to the community.

Key Factors

Biological factors (genes), Life experiences (trauma/abuse), and Family history of mental health problems all play a role.

Distress Signals

Emotional

Feeling sad/down, confused thinking, excessive fears, extreme mood changes.

Physical

Low energy, sleeping problems, significant tiredness, delusions or hallucinations.

Myths vs Facts

Myth: Mental health problems don't affect me.

Fact: They are common.

Self Help

Connect with others, stay positive, get physically active, help others, get enough sleep, and develop coping skills.
